My background doesn't fit a single label. I've taught university students, contributed to NLP research, built data visualisation dashboards, done user research, and shipped web products for clients. The thread connecting all of it is a genuine interest in how people perceive and interact with information — and a preference for understanding things by building them.

Right now that interest is pointed at visual computing and imaging science through my Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's in Imaging and Light Science in Extended Reality — studying across four universities in Finland, Belgium, France, and Japan. XR is the current focus; it won't be the last one.

The Three.js experiments on this page are me following one thread of that curiosity. Somewhere between studying and shipping, things start to make sense.
